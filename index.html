💡 Key Climatological Findings

Critical scientific takeaways from the 270-year observational record

Unprecedented Warming Acceleration: The warming rate over the last 40 years (+0.25°C/decade) is over 6.5 times faster than the early industrial rate (+0.038°C/decade).
Land Heats Faster than Oceans: Global land surface temperature has already warmed by +2.04°C above pre-industrial levels, while ocean surface has warmed by ~+0.9°C due to water's massive thermal capacity.
Shrinking Observational Uncertainty: Global measurement uncertainty dropped from ±3.57°C in 1750 down to ±0.03°C today due to modern satellites, weather stations, and Argo ocean buoys.
